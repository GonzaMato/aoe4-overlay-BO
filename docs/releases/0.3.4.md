# AoE4 Overlay 0.3.4

- Added a two-step build picker: choose a civilization by its flag, then choose one of its builds. Back returns to civilizations without changing your active build; Close cancels.
- Open it from Home or with the configurable Ctrl+Alt+O shortcut while AoE4 is in the foreground. Selection is manual and does not rely on civilization detection.
- Supports mouse and keyboard navigation: arrows and Enter, Escape to go back or close. The picker closes when you switch to another app.
- Choosing a different build starts at its first step and preserves the elapsed practice time. Confirming the current build keeps your current step.
- Includes English and Spanish labels, grouped civilization aliases, and all of your existing builds.
