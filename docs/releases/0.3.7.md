# AoE4 Overlay 0.3.7

- The app has a new identifier, `app.aoe4overlay`. Your data now lives in `%APPDATA%\app.aoe4overlay`, and Windows lists the publisher as **AoE4 Overlay**.
- Updating keeps everything: on first launch the app moves your builds, settings and practice history from the previous folder (`com.gonzalo.aoe4-overlay`) and removes the old folders. The installer also removes the old registry key.
- Choose **Exit app** before installing, as usual. If you go back to 0.3.6 afterwards, restore your data from a backup (**Settings → Data**), because 0.3.6 reads the old folder.
